Ask a Question
How do I book an appointment?
+

You can book directly through our Registration page, call us at +94 77 123 4567, or fill out the contact form above and our team will confirm your slot.

What are your working hours?
+

We're open Monday to Friday 8:00 AM - 8:00 PM, Saturday 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM, and Sunday 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM. Our emergency line is available 24/7.

Do you handle emergency veterinary cases?
+

Yes, our emergency line +94 77 999 0000 is available around the clock for urgent medical situations.

Can I visit before booking a service?
+

Absolutely. We welcome walk-in visits during working hours so you can see our facility and meet our team before booking.

How soon will I get a reply to my message?
+

We typically respond to emails and contact form submissions within 24 hours, and often much sooner during working hours.
