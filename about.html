About PetSphere

At PetSphere, we believe every pet deserves exceptional care, love, and attention. Our experienced team is dedicated to providing professional boarding, grooming, veterinary care, and training in a safe and welcoming environment.

Explore Services Contact Us

Our Story

PetSphere was founded with one simple dream—to create a place where pets receive the same love, care, and attention as they do at home. We understand that every pet is a cherished member of the family, which is why we treat them with compassion, patience, and professionalism.

Over the years, we have grown into a trusted pet care center, providing boarding, grooming, veterinary services, and training for hundreds of happy pets across Sri Lanka.
